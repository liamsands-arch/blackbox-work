# /brag plan: VG Brain

**What it is:** VG Brain is Voyageur Group's team memory, inside Claude: save the work worth keeping, and find it again later, even if a teammate wrote it.
**Who it's for:** everyone at VG doing client work in Claude. It saves them re-figuring out what a teammate already solved.
**What sets it apart:** every answer cites who said it and when; nothing saves on its own; a Friday Slack DM suggests what's worth keeping from your week and you pick where it goes.
**Most impressive claim:** ask before you start from scratch, and the answer comes back with a name and a date.
**Visual hook:** a real question typed in, then an answer with a citation, on VG's own sand/navy palette.
**Real UI/flow:** ask → cited answer → save on purpose (to a team) → Friday review DM with the team dropdown and Done.
**Tone:** `default` leaning `polished`: confident, clean, internal launch.
**Share caption:** "Before you start from scratch, ask the brain. Every answer says who figured it out and when."

**Identity (from the repo):** Sand #F1F0EA, Navy #172D36, Bleu #6C95A7, Growth #31BA93, Sall #E5486B, Snow #FFFFFF, Black #0B0B0B. Bricolage Grotesque (headings), Archivo (body). The canoe mark (vg-mark.png).

**Copy used from the product:** "Your team's memory, inside Claude." · "Save the work worth keeping. Find it again later, even if a teammate wrote it." · "Nothing saves on its own." · "How did we handle the Q3 line review last year?" · "Save how we handle the Q4 margin override, and why." (all from welcome.html) · the Friday review DM layout and its "I suggested a team for each (★)" line (slack-app.ts).

**Stand-ins:** people's names (Jordan Reyes, Sam Ortiz), the answer text, the review items and the "14 people" count are fictional. No client names beyond Target, no real notes.

**Music:** 120 BPM, A minor (Am–F–C–G, one chord per bar = 2s). Every cut lands on a beat.
